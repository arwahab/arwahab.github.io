About the founder

The story behind Wahab Waypoint — and the guy who built it.

Abdul Rafee Wahab

Tech Leader · Software Engineer · Architect

I'm a technical architect, lead software engineer, and tech leader. In my day job, I lead the Cloud Analytic Data Strategy group at a Fortune 50 company. I'm a problem solver both at heart and by choice.

Wahab Waypoint grew out of the work I do every day: using key domain knowledge and technology to solve business problems, and leading effective, purpose-driven engineering teams. It is the architecture intelligence I wished I had — packaged as a product line that pairs domain understanding, architectural evaluation, and fit review.

When I am not architecting systems, I write how-to tutorials on engineering and leadership, reading Marcus Aurelius' and Seneca's narratives, and fixing mechanical watches.

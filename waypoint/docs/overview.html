Wahab Waypoint, at a glance

Wahab Waypoint is a product line of three browser-based tools that approach one question from three angles: which architecture should I run? Each product works standalone, but they are designed to be used as a single journey that carries context from one step to the next.

3 products 18 workloads 10 reference architectures 8 evaluation criteria 12 domains

The three products

Product Answers Output
Domain Context Learner What does the business actually care about? A reusable domain profile
Architect Which architecture best fits this workload and priorities? Recommendation, tradeoffs, blueprint, ADR
Reviewer How well does the design I have fit the requirements? Fit score, strengths, gaps, watch-outs

How they fit together

The connector between the products is the domain profile. When you complete Domain Context Learner, it saves a profile — your domain, the qualities you ranked, and the shape of your data — in the browser.

Architect reads that profile automatically. Your priority sliders start pre-tuned toward what your business told us matters, instead of a generic default. Reviewer applies the same domain adjustments, so the requirements you are scored against match the context Architect used.

Learn the domain

Domain Context Learner builds the profile. One minute of input, reused everywhere.

Set priorities & evaluate

Architect tunes its weights from the profile, then lets you refine them for a specific workload.

Review what you run

Reviewer scores your existing or planned architecture against the same, domain-adjusted requirements.

Core vocabulary

Term Meaning
Domain The business you operate in, e.g. E-Commerce & Retail or Financial Services.
Scenario / workload A concrete system shape, e.g. E-Commerce Order Platform or Event Streaming Platform.
Reference architecture One of 10 real patterns, e.g. Apache Kafka, AWS Lambda, or Amazon EventBridge.
Evaluation criterion One of 8 ways architectures are scored — scalability, reliability, operational simplicity, cost efficiency, latency, ordering, replay, and compliance.
Domain profile Your saved context, shared between the three products.
💡

Everything runs in your browser. Domain profiles and theme preferences live in local storage — no account, no server, no uploads.

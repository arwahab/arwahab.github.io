How it works

One shared context, end to end.

The three products are designed as a single journey — from business understanding to architecture decision.

1

Learn the domain

In Domain Context Learner, capture your business domain, the qualities that matter most, and the shape of your data.

2

Set your priorities

In Architect, priority sliders start pre-tuned from your learned domain profile — then you refine them to taste.

3

Get the recommendation

Generate an evidence-based architecture recommendation, with alternatives, tradeoffs, blueprints, and decision records.

4

Review what you run

In Reviewer, score the architecture you already have — or plan to build — against the same workload requirements and see exactly where it falls short.
